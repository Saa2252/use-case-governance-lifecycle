Purpose, users, the data it sees — and the question most intake forms miss: what functions can it actually call?

Risk tier

How much could go wrong?

Not “is AI risky”. Eight questions: money, autonomy, reversibility, blast radius, PII, EU exposure, untrusted input, widest permission held.

The rule that fired for Ava

Untrusted member text reaches a model holding write access to money. Prompt injection stops being a content problem and becomes a payments problem.

Controls · the gate that stops things

What must be true before launch?

NOT PASSED — 2 of 14 controls not in place
  • The $50 reversal ceiling was a sentence in the system prompt, not a check in code.
  • Reversals hit the ledger with no record of which agent decision caused them.

Three weeks from launch. The board question had no answer, because nothing was being written down.

Time cost90 minutes
In the roomRisk, security, legal, ops
4
Evidence

Show me proof

A control nobody can point at is a claim. Every required control needs a dated artefact with a named owner.

  • Adversarial test report — 47 attempts, 3 findings, all closed
  • Threshold config export — service configuration, not prompt text
  • Reversal register — reconciled to the ledger, 5 consecutive nights
Time cost2 weeks elapsed
Undated evidenceIs not evidence

Approval

Who signs?

Accountable executiveHead of Member Operations✓
RiskChief Risk Officer✓
Legal / data protectionGeneral Counsel and DPO✓

One named person, not a committee. A committee cannot be held to account — when it goes wrong, the honest answer to “who decided this” becomes “the room”.

RuleNo signature, no deploy
Signed againstA frozen control set
6
Monitoring

Is it still safe?

Approval is a snapshot of a system that will not stay still.

  • Metrics with consequences — a metric with no consequence is a chart
  • The 2am path — who is paged, what they may do without waking an exec
  • Model change control — the supplier moves the version, gate 2 re-runs light
  • A retirement trigger — systems without one get quietly inherited
Time cost30 min, then 30 min quarterly
The point of the whole thing

A lifecycle that cannot say no is paperwork.

Condition 1 — due 20 Oct

Threshold moved out of the prompt into service configuration and lowered to $25. Reversals between $25 and $50 queue for human approval.

Condition 2 — due 27 Oct

Every reversal writes to an append-only register the agent credential cannot modify. Reconciled to the ledger nightly.

Go-live moved from 5 October to 2 November. Then the gate was re-run.

ResultApproved with conditions, 31 Oct
